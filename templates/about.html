A LITTLE ABOUT THIS PLACE ↓

Tsukuyomi was imagined as a small corner of the internet where memories, reflections, and unfinished thoughts could be given room to breathe.

Not every feeling needs an immediate answer. Not every letter needs to be sent. Sometimes, writing is simply a way to understand what we carry.

02 / A PLACE FOR UNSENT WORDS

Read a reflection, stay for a while, or enter the Writing Room to write something of your own. Your letters are saved here on this application until you choose what to do with them.

Saving a letter does not send it to anyone. Sealing a letter keeps it from being edited or deleted; it does not deliver the letter.

04 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE MOON

A Letter from a Wanderer

I have come to think that some of the things that shape us arrive quietly. They find us in passing moments, in words left unspoken, and in the presence of people who may never fully know the place they have come to hold in our thoughts.

Sometimes, distance gives us room to reflect. We begin to notice the things we feel, the memories we keep, and the parts of ourselves we are still learning to understand. Not every question finds an answer, and not every feeling needs to be explained before it can mean something.

Perhaps not every encounter is meant to lead somewhere. Some become quiet reminders that our lives can be changed by moments we never expected, and by people whose paths only briefly meet our own. There is gratitude in that, even when we cannot know what those moments were meant to become.

Tsukuyomi grew from a wish to give those thoughts somewhere to rest. A place for the words we carry, the memories we return to, and the letters that may never reach their intended destination. Here, perhaps, they can be understood a little better.

If you have found your way here, I hope you find room for your own words, too. You do not need to know exactly what they mean yet. You do not need to send them anywhere. Sometimes, it is enough to write them down and let yourself see them beneath a different light.

Perhaps some things are meant to pass beneath the same moon, leaving us changed long after their light has gone.

— A Wanderer Passing Through

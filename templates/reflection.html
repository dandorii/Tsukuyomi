Things the Moon Remembers

A REFLECTION UNDER THE MOONLIGHT

Some memories arrive quietly, like moonlight finding its way through a half-open window.

They remind us of places we have been, people we have met, and moments that seemed ordinary until time gave them meaning.

Perhaps the things we remember are not always the things we meant to keep. Sometimes, they are simply the moments that helped shape who we became.
